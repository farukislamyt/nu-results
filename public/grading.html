Degree Pass GPA

When National University supplies a GPA, this viewer displays that value directly and does not invent or recalculate a Degree Pass GPA.

Master's result values

Master's responses are displayed from the fields returned by the National University result service. Where a response contains CGPA, GPA or course grades, the viewer presents those returned values.
