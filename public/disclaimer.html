Disclaimer

NU Results is an independent result-viewing interface intended to make available National University Bangladesh result information easier to request and read. It is not the official National University website.

Independent service

NU Results is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or the official website of National University, Bangladesh.

Result information

This interface requests result information through the National University result workflow and presents the returned information for convenience. We do not issue, alter, certify or guarantee academic results, certificates, transcripts or examination decisions.

Accuracy and verification

Displayed information can depend on the National University result service, the examination details entered by the user, CAPTCHA completion, network conditions and other technical factors. Verify important academic information through an official source before relying on it.

Availability

The service may be unavailable when the National University result server is under maintenance, a result is not published, entered information is incorrect, a CAPTCHA cannot be completed, or a network request fails.

Official source

For official records and verification, use the National University result portal directly.
