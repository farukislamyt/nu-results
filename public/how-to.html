How to Check NU Result

  1. Select your examination type: Honours 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th Year or Consolidated Result.
  2. Enter the examination year, exam roll and registration number exactly as shown on your documents.
  3. Enter the displayed CAPTCHA answer.
  4. Tap View Result. The website retrieves the result from the National University result service.
  5. Use Print / PDF to print or save the displayed result as a PDF from your browser.

For important academic or official use, always verify the information on the official NU result portal.
