About NU Results

NU Results is an independent interface designed to make available National University Bangladesh result information easier to request and read on mobile, tablet and desktop devices.

What this service does

The service provides result-search forms for Honours, Degree Pass and Master's examinations and presents information returned by the National University result service.

  • Uses the National University result workflow and current CAPTCHA process.
  • Displays returned student, course, grade and GPA information when supplied by the source.
  • Provides print, PDF, share and copy conveniences where available.
  • Keeps recent searches in the browser for convenience.

What this service is not

This is not the official website of National University. NU Results does not issue, alter, certify or guarantee academic records.

Official verification

For official records, verification and academic decisions, consult National University services directly.
