Privacy Policy

This page explains how information is handled when you use NU Results to request National University Bangladesh result information.

Result searches

When you submit a result search, the required examination details are sent to this service so it can request the result from the National University result service. The service does not intentionally maintain a permanent database of individual student searches.

Browser-stored recent searches

The interface may store recent searches locally in your browser for convenience. This storage is controlled on your device.

Temporary sessions

The result workflow uses a temporary encrypted session. Sessions are short-lived and expire automatically.

Information you should not enter

Do not enter passwords, payment information or unrelated sensitive information into the result form.

Official verification

For official records, certificates, transcripts and academic verification, use the National University service directly.
