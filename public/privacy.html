Privacy

This site is an independent result viewer and is not the National University website.

  • Search details are sent to this service only to request the selected result from the NU result server.
  • The site does not intentionally maintain a permanent database of students' result searches.
  • Recent search entries are stored locally in your browser so you can reuse them; you can clear them from the search page.
  • The temporary encrypted session used for the NU request expires automatically.
  • Do not enter passwords, payment information or unrelated personal information into this site.

For the official result service, use the National University result portal.
