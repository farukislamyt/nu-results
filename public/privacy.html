Privacy

Privacy Policy

This page explains how information is handled when you use NU Results to request National University Bangladesh result information.

How result searches are handled

When you submit a result search, the examination details required by the selected workflow are sent to this service so it can request the result from the National University result service. The service does not intentionally maintain a permanent database of individual student result searches.

Browser-stored recent searches

The interface may store recent searches locally in your browser so you can reuse details more conveniently. This browser storage is controlled on your device and can be cleared from the search interface or browser settings.

Temporary request sessions

The result workflow uses a temporary encrypted session for a request. Such sessions are short-lived and expire automatically rather than becoming a permanent personal profile.

Information you should not enter

Do not enter passwords, payment information or unrelated sensitive information into the result form. Use only the examination information required by the National University workflow.

Third-party and official services

Result requests depend on the National University result service. Links to official National University websites take you to external services governed by their own policies and practices.

Official verification

For official records, certificates, transcripts and academic verification, use the National University service directly.
