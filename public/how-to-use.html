How to Check Your National University Result

Use this guide to check available Honours, Degree Pass or Master's results.

Before you start

Keep your examination year, roll, registration number and current CAPTCHA answer ready.

Steps

  1. Select Honours, Degree Pass or Master's.
  2. Choose the correct Examination.
  3. Enter the four-digit Examination Year.
  4. Enter the Exam Roll when required or accepted.
  5. Enter your Registration Number.
  6. Enter the CAPTCHA answer.
  7. Select View Result.

If a result does not appear

  • Confirm the examination and year.
  • Check the roll and registration number digit by digit.
  • Refresh the CAPTCHA and enter the new answer.
  • If the NU server is busy, wait briefly before trying again.
  • For official verification, use the National University result portal directly.

Official verification

This interface is for convenience. For academic decisions, certificates, transcripts or official records, verify the information through the National University result service.
