How to Check Your NU Result

A simple guide to using this independent result-viewing interface.

Steps

  1. Select the correct Examination.
  2. Enter the Examination Year.
  3. Enter your Exam Roll.
  4. Enter your Registration Number.
  5. Enter the answer shown in the CAPTCHA.
  6. Select View Result and wait for the NU result server response.

If the result does not appear

  • Check the examination and year.
  • Check the roll and registration number carefully.
  • Check the CAPTCHA answer.
  • If the NU server is slow, wait and try again without repeatedly refreshing.

Important

This site does not replace the official National University result portal. For official verification, academic decisions, or records, always check the result through the National University portal.
