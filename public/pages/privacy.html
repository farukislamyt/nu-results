Privacy

This site is an independent result-viewing interface and is not the National University website.

How searches are handled

  • Search details are sent to this service only to request the selected result from the NU result server.
  • The service does not intentionally maintain a permanent database of student result searches.
  • Recent searches are stored locally in your browser so you can reuse them. You can clear them from the search page.
  • The temporary encrypted session used for an NU request expires automatically.
  • Do not enter passwords, payment information, or unrelated personal information into this site.

Third-party result service

Result information is retrieved from the National University result portal. For official records and verification, use the official service.
