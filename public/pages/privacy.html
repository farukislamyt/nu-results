Privacy

Your search, handled simply

Clear information about what happens when you use the result viewer.

How searches are handled

  • Search details are sent to this service to request the selected result from the NU result server.
  • The service does not intentionally maintain a permanent database of student result searches.
  • Recent searches are stored locally in your browser so you can reuse them; you can clear them from the search page.
  • The temporary encrypted session used for an NU request expires automatically.

What you should not enter

Do not enter passwords, payment information or unrelated sensitive information. Use only the examination information required by the NU result workflow.

Official result service

Result information is retrieved through the National University result portal. For official records and verification, use the official service directly.
