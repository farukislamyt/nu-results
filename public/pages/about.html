About NU Results

NU Results is an independent, mobile-friendly interface designed to make it easier to request and read result information returned by the National University result server.

What this service does

  • Provides a simple result-search interface.
  • Supports the available Honours examination categories exposed by the NU result form.
  • Presents returned student and course information in a readable format.
  • Offers convenient print, PDF, share and copy actions.

What this service is not

This is not the official website of National University. It does not issue, modify, or certify academic results.

Official source

For official results, notices, records and academic verification, use the National University services directly.
