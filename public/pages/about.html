About NU Results

A focused interface for reading result information returned by the National University result service.

What this service does

NU Results provides a simpler way to request and read available National University Honours and Degree Pass result information on mobile, tablet and desktop.

  • Uses the official NU result workflow and CAPTCHA.
  • Presents returned student and course information in a readable layout.
  • Provides print, PDF, share and copy conveniences.
  • Keeps recent searches in the browser rather than maintaining a personal search history here.

What this service is not

This is not the official website of National University. It does not issue, alter, certify or guarantee academic results.

Official source

For official records, verification and academic decisions, always use the National University services directly.
