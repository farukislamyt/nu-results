About NU Results

NU Results is an independent interface designed to make available National University Bangladesh result information easier to request and read on mobile, tablet and desktop devices.

What this service does

The service provides result-search forms for Honours, Degree Pass and Master's examinations. It collects only the examination information needed by the selected National University result workflow and presents the response returned by that service.

  • Uses the National University result workflow and current CAPTCHA process.
  • Displays returned student, course, grade and GPA information when supplied by the source.
  • Provides practical print, PDF, share and copy conveniences where available.
  • Keeps recent searches in the browser for convenience rather than maintaining a permanent personal search history here.

What this service is not

This is not the official website of National University. NU Results does not issue, alter, certify or guarantee academic records, certificates, transcripts or examination decisions.

Official source and verification

For official records, verification and academic decisions, users should consult National University services directly. If information displayed by this interface differs from an official record, the official source should be treated as authoritative.
