Calculated GPA

The displayed GPA is a credit-weighted calculation based on the letter grades and credits parsed from the result returned by the NU result page. It is provided for convenience and should not be treated as an independently issued official GPA.

For official academic decisions, verify the result and applicable regulations with National University.
