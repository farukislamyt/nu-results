Grading & GPA

Understand how this viewer presents grade points and GPA information.

Honours grade-point mapping

For Honours results, the viewer calculates a credit-weighted GPA from the course credits and letter grades parsed from the NU result response.

Degree Pass GPA

Degree Pass result sheets may contain a GPA supplied by National University, including year-specific labels such as GPA (2nd Year). When NU provides a GPA, this viewer displays that value directly.

The viewer does not invent or recalculate a Degree Pass GPA.

Important

Displayed grades and GPA information are provided for convenience. Always verify important academic information with National University and the applicable official regulations.
